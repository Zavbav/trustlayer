Know exactly what to do.

TrustLayer is a review assistant. It checks draft text for selected warning patterns. It does not approve a response or prove that it is safe.

TL

Open the scanner

Choose Response scanner in the left menu, or tap Scanner in the bottom menu on a phone.

Where to go Response scanner

What to enter A draft reply

Run the check

04

Read the flags

Important to know

TrustLayer currently uses transparent browser-based rules, not an AI model. It cannot confirm that a reply follows every company policy. Add exact trigger phrases, review every flag, and have a person approve the final response.
